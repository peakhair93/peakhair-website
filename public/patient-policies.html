Appointment policy

To help secure your desired date and time, we suggest booking a few weeks in advance. Please confirm current availability with Peak. A credit card is required to reserve an appointment.

Consultations policy

We offer free consultations and an in-depth hair-loss and donor-area assessment with a provider. We want you to understand your options and have realistic expectations before deciding on treatment. We will discuss your goals, available options now and over time, and a proposed plan suited to your situation. There is a limit of two (2) free consultations per person in any 365-day period.

Cancellation and late-arrival policy

For individual appointments, please give at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. A $100 charge applies if you cancel or reschedule less than twenty-four (24) hours before your appointment or do not attend.

For group appointments, please give at least seventy-two (72) hours' notice. The card on file will be charged $200 if a group appointment is canceled less than seventy-two (72) hours in advance.

If you arrive more than ten (10) minutes late, you may be asked to reschedule to avoid disrupting other patients' appointments.

Pricing and prepayments

Prices for products and services may change at any time. Once prepaid, a purchased service must be redeemed within one (1) year of purchase.

If a client's credit or debit card or CareCredit payment, where applicable, has a chargeback, fraud issue or other payment problem, Peak may stop accepting those payment methods from that client and require cash payment.

Your individual treatment, total price and what is included should be confirmed in a written quote before you pay.

Returns and refunds

If you purchase a skincare product from Peak, you may return it in its original packaging, unopened, within thirty (30) days of purchase. We do not accept returns of opened or used products. Gift cards, where offered, cannot be returned or exchanged.

Prepayments are refundable within ten (10) days of purchase. Services already received cannot be refunded. Payments for training courses, if offered, are non-refundable.

Contact info@peakhairtransplant.com for help with a return or refund.

Children and pets

Children

For children's safety and the comfort of patients, please arrange childcare while you receive services. An adult must accompany anyone under eighteen (18) receiving services.

Pets

For the health and safety of patients, pets are not permitted. Working service animals are welcome.
