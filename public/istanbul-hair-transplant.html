What happens before a transplant?

There is no responsible way to promise a graft count or a result before an individual clinical assessment.

Discuss your hair loss

Review your pattern, history and expectations. A clinician can tell you if an additional evaluation is needed.

Assess donor hair

Donor capacity is limited. The number and placement of grafts must be planned around your own hair.

Design a natural hairline

Discuss your facial features, age and possible future thinning before deciding on a surgical approach.

Treatment options

FUE and careful graft planning

Peak discusses FUE techniques, including Sapphire FUE, as part of a hair-restoration plan. “Sapphire” describes a blade used to prepare recipient sites; it does not guarantee density, speed of healing or a specific outcome.

Ask which steps your clinician will perform, how grafts are handled and what follow-up care is included. Explore Peak's FUE techniques.

Travel planning

Know what your visit includes

Ask for your procedure location, clinician, written treatment quote and a clear breakdown of any hotel, transfers, medications and follow-up arrangements before making travel plans. Package inclusions can vary; confirm them in writing with Peak.

Plan around your clinician's own pre- and post-operative instructions, not a generic travel schedule. Read our pre-op checklist and post-op guide.

Questions to ask

Before you decide

Who treats me?

Confirm the licensed clinician, the address of the treating facility and who performs each step of the procedure.

What does my quote cover?

Request written confirmation of treatment and any travel support or aftercare included in your individual plan.

What happens afterward?

Ask who to contact about clinical concerns after returning home and when follow-up will take place.
