A considered plan

What happens before a transplant?

A consultation is the place to establish the cause of your thinning, whether you have suitable donor hair and what an age-appropriate hairline could look like. Not every patient needs a transplant now.

Understand your loss

Review your history, pattern of thinning and goals. If the cause is unclear, a clinician can advise whether further assessment is needed before surgery.

Assess donor capacity

Follicles are moved from an existing donor area. The available supply is limited, so graft estimates must follow an individual assessment.

Plan for the future

Hairline placement should make sense alongside your facial features and possible future thinning of untreated hair.

Treatment options

Sapphire FUE, explained

Peak offers Sapphire FUE in its hair restoration program. In FUE, individual follicular units are taken from a donor area and placed into thinning areas. “Sapphire” describes the blade used to make small recipient sites; it does not guarantee a particular result or replace careful planning.

Depending on the cause and stage of hair loss, our team can also discuss non-surgical options such as PRP. Any recommendation should follow a clinical assessment, not a preset graft count.

Recovery and follow-up

Growth takes time

A transplant moves hair but does not stop untreated hair from continuing to thin. Follow your own surgeon's aftercare instructions. The American Academy of Dermatology says transplanted hairs commonly shed 2–8 weeks after surgery; many patients see results in 6–9 months and some take 12 months. Your timeline may differ.

Common questions

Before you decide

How many grafts will I need?

The size of the thinning area, hair characteristics and safe donor supply all affect the plan. A clinician needs to assess these before giving a meaningful estimate.

Is Sapphire FUE the right choice for everyone?

No. The blade is one part of the procedure. First establish the diagnosis and whether transplantation is suitable for your situation.

Will my results look like the photos?

Before-and-after images show individual outcomes, not a promise. Ask for a plan based on your own hair and donor capacity.

Can I ask about cost during the consultation?

Yes. Ask for a written, personalized quote that explains the recommended treatment, what it includes and any aftercare. The website does not list a fixed New York transplant price.
