The short answer

What does a hair transplant cost in New York City?

Hair transplants in New York City typically cost between $4,000 and $15,000, according to price ranges collected by GoodRx. Across the US, the same source puts the average at $6,000 to $12,000. Where you land depends mostly on how many grafts you need and what the price includes.

At Peak, you get a free assessment first, then a written quote showing the technique, the graft plan and exactly what is included, before you decide anything. Monthly payment plans start at $149/month through Cherry.

What drives the price

Six things that change your total

Two quotes with the same headline price can include very different things. These are the factors to compare.

Number of grafts

The size of the area you want to restore and your donor supply decide the graft count, the biggest single cost driver. Suitable patients may be planned for up to 5,000 grafts in one session.

Technique

Sapphire FUE, DHI and unshaven FUE involve different tools and time, which affects price.

Who does the work

Ask who designs your hairline and who performs each surgical step. The clinical team's experience is part of what you are paying for.

What's included

Medication, aftercare products, PRP and follow-up visits may or may not be in a quote. Ask for a line-by-line list.

Number of sessions

Advanced hair loss may need more than one session. A good plan explains whether a second session is likely.

Aftercare

Ask how long follow-up lasts and what it covers. Growth takes up to a year, so support through that period matters.

Why do hair transplant prices vary so much?

The biggest drivers are the number of grafts, the technique (for example Sapphire FUE or DHI), who performs each step, and whether medication, aftercare and follow-up are included. Compare written quotes line by line, not headline prices.
