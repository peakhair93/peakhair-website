Hair transplant surgeon · Peak NYC

J. Jimenez, MD

Board-Certified Family Medicine Physician & Hair Transplant Surgeon

Dr. J. Jimenez is a board-certified Family Medicine physician and hair transplant surgeon with more than 10 years of medical experience. At Peak Hair Restoration, he combines his medical background with a personalized approach to hair restoration, emphasizing patient safety, thoughtful planning and natural-looking results.

Originally from Staten Island, New York, Dr. Jimenez completed residency training at Jackson Memorial Hospital in Miami, followed by further training at Saint Joseph's Medical Center, a New York Medical College affiliate in Yonkers. He also serves as Section Chief of Hospital Medicine at Sharon Hospital.

Dr. Jimenez is dedicated to understanding each patient's individual needs and helping them feel informed and supported throughout their care. Outside of medicine, he enjoys spending time with his family and exploring his passion for cooking.

Simon Naparstek, MD

Urologist & Hair Transplant Surgeon · More than 50 years in medicine

Dr. Simon Naparstek brings more than five decades of medical and surgical experience to Peak Hair Restoration. A urologist based in New York City, he applies the precision and clinical judgment of a long surgical career to hair transplantation.

Dr. Naparstek earned his medical degree from the University of Zurich in Switzerland and completed his residency at SUNY Health Science Center at Brooklyn (SUNY Downstate).

For patients, that depth of experience means careful assessment, experienced surgical care and honest advice about what a hair transplant can and cannot achieve.

How we work

Physician-led hair restoration in NYC

Every hair transplant plan at Peak starts with a medical assessment, not a sales pitch. Your hair loss, scalp, donor area and goals are evaluated before any technique or graft count is recommended.

  • Diagnosis first. Some types of hair loss need medical treatment before a transplant is considered.
  • Hairline design around your face, age and donor capacity.
  • Techniques including Sapphire FUE, DHI, beard transplants and PRP.
  • Care for every hair type, including Afro-textured hair.
  • Local follow-up at our Midtown Manhattan clinic.
