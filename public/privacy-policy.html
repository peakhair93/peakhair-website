Privacy policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains the information collected through this website and when you contact Peak for scheduling and coordination. For questions or requests, email info@peakhairtransplant.com.

What we collect

When you use the consultation form or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, preferred location, preferred contact method, treatment interest and timing for a callback. The general website form is for scheduling only: please do not submit medical history, photographs, payment-card details or other sensitive health information. Contact the medical practice for an appropriate way to share clinical information.

As you use the site, technical and usage data may be collected automatically: IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and browser type or identifiers, referring pages, pages requested or visited, clicks, interactions and search terms where available. Our site includes a Ryze measurement script and may use browser storage or similar technologies for analytics.

How we use information

Peak Hair Restoration is a management company providing nonclinical scheduling, billing support, administration, IT, marketing and patient coordination to JC Jimenez Medical Health Wellness Services PLLC. Peak uses website inquiries to coordinate a callback or consultation, operate and protect the website, understand which pages are useful, and comply with applicable obligations. The medical practice provides all medical services; its licensed clinicians make evaluation and treatment decisions. We identify the purpose for collecting information before or at the time of collection and use it for that purpose or compatible purposes, subject to applicable law. Please provide accurate, current details when contacting us.

Service providers and disclosures

The website's consultation form is sent to Formspree, which processes the form submission so Peak can receive and respond to it. The site also uses Ryze for measurement and Google Fonts to display its typefaces. Website hosting and technical providers may process information to deliver and secure the site. We may disclose information when needed to provide the services you request, when required by law, or as otherwise permitted with appropriate notice or consent. External links, including Google Maps and social networks, are subject to those services' own privacy policies when you visit them.

JC Jimenez Medical Health Wellness Services PLLC provides medical care. Medical records created in connection with care are governed by the medical practice's applicable clinical privacy notice and legal requirements. Ask your treating clinic for that notice; this website policy does not replace it.

How long we retain it and how we protect it

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above and any applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements. We use reasonable safeguards against loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, misuse or modification. No online transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

To ask what information we hold, correct it, or request its deletion, email info@peakhairtransplant.com. We will review the request under applicable law and may need to verify your identity. Some information may need to be retained for medical, legal, accounting or security reasons. The public website does not provide a patient account to disable; if you have a separate patient portal, contact that clinic for its account options.

You can manage browser settings to limit cookies or similar storage, although this may affect site features. You can also choose not to provide information through the website form and contact Peak by email instead.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our services and practices change. The version posted here applies to this website. Questions about privacy or our data practices may be sent to info@peakhairtransplant.com.
