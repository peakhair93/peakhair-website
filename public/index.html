A guide, not a guarantee

The first 12 months of hair transplant recovery

Recovery has stages, but no two patients follow the same calendar. Here is an overview to discuss with your treating clinician—not a substitute for the personal instructions you receive after surgery.

  1. Procedure day

    Protect the new grafts

    Your medical team places the grafts and gives you a written care plan. Avoid rubbing or pressing on the treated area, and follow their directions for medicines and sleeping.

  2. First 2 weeks

    Gentle healing

    Tenderness, swelling or small crusts can occur. Ask your clinician when and how to wash; do not pick at scabs. Your individual activity limits come from your treating team.

  3. Months 1–3

    Shedding may happen

    Some transplanted hairs can shed while follicles enter a resting phase. That change does not by itself mean treatment failed. Contact your clinical team about symptoms that worry you.

  4. Months 3–6

    Early growth may appear

    New hairs may begin to emerge gradually and look fine or uneven at first. The timing differs by person and by area treated; keep any follow-up agreed with your clinician.

  5. Months 6–9

    Watch texture and coverage change

    Some patients see more visible coverage as hair matures. Progress can be slower in some areas. Compare photographs taken in similar lighting rather than judging day to day.

  6. Months 9–12 and beyond

    Review your progress

    Review your result and any continuing native-hair loss with your treating clinician. Further maturation can occur after month 12; a final appearance or density is never guaranteed on a fixed date.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Most people with stable donor hair are candidates, but the honest answer requires an assessment. Our free consultation reviews your hair loss stage, donor capacity and health history before any recommendation is made.

Sapphire FUE is a surgical transplant — grafts are moved from your donor area to thinning zones using sapphire-blade micro-incisions. PRP is non-surgical: platelet-rich plasma from your own blood is injected to strengthen follicles you still have. Many patients combine both, using PRP to support transplant results.

New growth typically begins around month 3–4, visible density arrives by month 6–8, and final results settle between months 12 and 14.

Procedures are performed under local anaesthesia. Most patients describe mild discomfort only during the initial numbing, and return to normal routines within days.

All-inclusive packages cover the procedure, hotel accommodation, VIP airport transfers, medication and aftercare kit, plus a 12-month follow-up programme. Details are confirmed in your consultation.
